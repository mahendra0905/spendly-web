Spendly Privacy Policy

lock 100% Local & Privacy-First Architecture

1. Local Storage Only: Spendly is designed to operate completely offline. All your expenses, budgets, friend ledger records, and settings are stored locally on your device using native browser IndexedDB and localStorage.

2. No External Tracking: We do not collect, transmit, or sell any of your personal financial data. No analytics trackers, pixel tags, or third-party telemetries are embedded.

3. Complete Data Control: You own your data. You can export your data into CSV/Excel or JSON backup files at any time, or permanently clear all stored data with a single click.

4. Cloud & Sync: Restoring backups is done locally by importing your JSON file. Your sensitive financial information never leaves your browser.
